About

I am a PhD student at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) and Peng Cheng Laboratory (PCL), working under the supervision of Prof. Hong Zhang. My research focuses on Visual Place Recognition (VPR), geo-localization, and learning-based computer vision.

Previously, I gained industry experience at Huawei and conducted research at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA). I received my B.Eng. from China University of Mining and Technology (CUMT).
