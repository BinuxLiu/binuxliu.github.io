Ph.D. student in Intelligent Manufacturing & Robotics · SUSTech

I am a Ph.D. student at SUSTech, advised by Prof. Hong Zhang (Chair Professor; Fellow of the Canadian Academy of Engineering and IEEE), and expect to graduate in February 2027. I received my master’s degree from UCAS, advised by Prof. Yihong Wu (Research Professor at the Institute of Automation, CAS; doctoral supervisor).

My research focuses on visual localization, 3D perception, and embodied intelligence. I am currently an intern at EngineAI, working on embodied data pipelines and visual foundation models.
