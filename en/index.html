Ph.D. student in Intelligent Manufacturing & Robotics · SUSTech

I am a Ph.D. student at SUSTech, advised by Prof. Hong Zhang, and expect to graduate in February 2027. I received my master’s degree from UCAS, advised by Prof. Yihong Wu.

My research focuses on visual localization, 3D perception, and embodied intelligence, with the goal of helping robots understand space and act reliably. I am currently an intern at EngineAI, working on embodied data pipelines and visual foundation models.
